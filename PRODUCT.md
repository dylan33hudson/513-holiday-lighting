# PRODUCT — 513 Holiday Lighting LLC

## What this page is

A single-page marketing site. One measurable job: convert a visitor into a quote request.
Everything else — aesthetics, copy, motion — exists only to serve that job.

## Business context

- **Stage:** Brand new. Founded by college entrepreneurship students in Cincinnati.
- **No track record yet.** No real reviews, no verified installation count, no press mentions.
  Stats bar is explicitly placeholder — update only with real numbers.
- **Seasonality:** The business operates Oct–Jan. The website competes in a narrow booking
  window. Urgency (limited slots) is a legitimate and honest motivator.
- **Market position:** Premium, not budget. Competing on quality, care, and professionalism —
  not price.

## Target audience

**Primary:** Homeowners in Cincinnati's affluent suburbs (Hyde Park, Indian Hill, Mason,
Anderson Township, etc.) who can afford professional services and want convenience.

**Secondary:** Commercial property managers who need reliable, insured contractors.

**Mindset on arrival:** They're not comparing prices — they're deciding whether to trust a
new company with their home and holiday season. Trust signals matter more than features.

## Core value proposition

*You enjoy the result. We do the work. Nothing to climb, untangle, or store.*

The emotional trade is: give up control of one seasonal chore → get back time, safety, and
a more beautiful result than you'd manage yourself.

## Content constraints

- Do NOT add invented testimonials, star ratings, review counts, or installation numbers.
- Do NOT present AI-generated or stock houses as our work. Gallery, testimonials, and before/after
  slots take real installs only. Until the first 2026 installs go up in November, the gallery is an
  honest empty state that says so.
- Keep student-founded identity — it's authentic positioning (young, hungry, community-rooted).
- The hero copy follows the new hero direction, not the copy in the pre-2026 `index.html`:
  headline "Be the house the whole street slows down for.", lede "Custom-cut, commercial-grade
  Christmas lights — designed, installed, maintained all season, then taken down and stored by us.
  You pick the look on your roofline. We handle every ladder, clip, and timer.", CTAs "Get a free
  estimate" and "See last season's installs", and the trust line "Fully insured · Takedown +
  storage included · Installs begin [NOV 1]". Bracketed values are placeholders to confirm.
- Outside the hero, keep existing copy verbatim unless fixing a grammatical error. Where the
  quote-form design board supplies copy (pricing, form labels, error and success messages), that
  copy wins over the older `index.html` equivalents.
- Pricing is flat and public (updated Nov 2026): $6 per linear foot plus Ohio sales tax, $600 minimum
  (covers up to 100 ft), quoted on the actual roofline and fixed before install. Sales tax is always
  shown as "plus Ohio sales tax" and listed separately on invoices; Ohio law bars advertising
  tax-included prices. 25% books the date; the balance is due when the lights are up. There are no
  packages: the pricing section shows example homes (up to 100 ft $600, ~140 ft $840, ~250 ft
  $1,500, ~400 ft $2,400). Every light color is the same price. Add-ons (only with a roofline install): customer's
  own wreath hung free, supplied wreath $75, trunk wrap $95, full tree wrap under 10 ft $175 (no trees
  over 10 ft this season), bushes $55 per net light, gutter cleaning $149 (1 story) / $199 (2 story).
  Add-ons count toward the minimum. Standard colors are all the same price; twinkle bulbs cost
  extra. Rebook by September 1 locks the price for three seasons. This replaces
  the $850 minimum, the Classic/Signature/Estate packages, the 50% deposit, the $25 own-wreath fee,
  and the past-Jan-10 fee.
- Service area is Hamilton, Butler, Warren, and Clermont counties (West Chester is in Butler).
- The quote form submits to Netlify Forms (`data-netlify`), which also carries the photo upload.
  Notifications are configured in the Netlify dashboard, not in code.

## What success looks like

A visitor lands → reads 3–4 sections → submits the quote form or calls (330) 466-4011.
Everything on the page should accelerate that path or get out of the way.
