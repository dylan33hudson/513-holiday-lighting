# PRODUCT — 513 Holiday Lighting LLC

## What this page is

A single-page marketing site. One measurable job: convert a visitor into a quote request.
Everything else — aesthetics, copy, motion — exists only to serve that job.

## Business context

- **Stage:** Brand new. Founded by college entrepreneurship students in Cincinnati.
- **No track record yet.** No real reviews, no verified installation count, no press mentions.
  Stats bar is explicitly placeholder — update only with real numbers.
- **Seasonality:** The business operates Oct–Jan. The website competes in a narrow booking
  window. Urgency (limited slots) is a legitimate and honest motivator.
- **Market position:** Premium, not budget. Competing on quality, care, and professionalism —
  not price.

## Target audience

**Primary:** Homeowners in Cincinnati's affluent suburbs (Hyde Park, Indian Hill, Mason,
Anderson Township, etc.) who can afford professional services and want convenience.

**Secondary:** Commercial property managers who need reliable, insured contractors.

**Mindset on arrival:** They're not comparing prices — they're deciding whether to trust a
new company with their home and holiday season. Trust signals matter more than features.

## Core value proposition

*You enjoy the result. We do the work. Nothing to climb, untangle, or store.*

The emotional trade is: give up control of one seasonal chore → get back time, safety, and
a more beautiful result than you'd manage yourself.

## Page sections and their persuasion role

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| Hero | Hook + primary CTA. Must feel premium instantly. |
| Stats bar | Trust anchoring (leave as placeholder until real numbers exist). |
| Services | Reduces objection: "What exactly do you do?" Shows full lifecycle. |
| How It Works | Reduces friction: "Is this hard to get started?" Shows it's 4 easy steps. |
| Gallery | Proof of craft. Placeholder until real photos exist. |
| Why Choose Us | Differentiator vs. DIY or cheaper competitors. Licensed, warranted, local. |
| Testimonials | Social proof. Placeholder until real reviews collected. |
| Service Area | Filters intent: confirms relevance before the form. |
| Quote Form | Conversion. Low-friction: name + email + city is enough to get started. |
| CTA Banner | Last-chance urgency push before footer. |
| Footer | Contact, navigation, trust close. |

## Content constraints

- Do NOT add invented testimonials, star ratings, review counts, or installation numbers.
- Keep student-founded identity — it's authentic positioning (young, hungry, community-rooted).
- Keep all existing copy verbatim unless fixing a grammatical error.
- The quote form submits to Formspree — endpoint ID still needs to be replaced.

## What success looks like

A visitor lands → reads 3–4 sections → submits the quote form or calls (330) 466-4011.
Everything on the page should accelerate that path or get out of the way.
