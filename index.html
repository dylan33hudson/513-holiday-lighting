The Details

Exactly How
We Do It

No mystery, no guesswork. Here's precisely what happens from the first measurement to January takedown.

1

We measure your home to the foot

We walk and measure every roofline, peak, gable, and feature so your design — and your price — fit your house exactly, with nothing guessed.

2

Your lights are built in our shop

Each run is custom-cut and assembled to your measurements on weatherproof commercial wire. No sagging, gapping store-bought strands — your set fits your roofline like it was made for it, because it was.

3

Commercial-grade LED C9 bulbs

We use durable, warm-glowing C9 LED bulbs spaced every 12 inches for a smooth, continuous line of light — not the patchy, uneven look of consumer mini-lights.

4

Zero damage to your home

Bulbs clip onto your shingles and gutters with all-weather fasteners. No staples, no nails, no drilling, no holes — ever. When we take them down in January, there’s no trace we were on your roof but the memory of how good it looked.

5

Clean power, automatic timing

Runs connect with sealed, weatherproof plugs to a single outdoor GFCI outlet and a professional astronomical timer — it turns your display on at dusk and off overnight, on its own, all season. You never touch a switch.

6

Barely-there energy use

Because everything is LED, your display sips power — a typical home adds only a few dollars a month (see the exact numbers below).

7

We’re on call all season

A bulb goes dark or a timer drifts? One call and we’re back out to fix it — free, every time, all season long.

8

Takedown & safe storage

In January we remove everything and store your custom-built set for you. Next season is a quick reinstall at your locked-in renewal rate — no measuring, no rebuilding.

LED or Incandescent?

We install commercial-grade C9 bulbs in either style. LED is our default recommendation, but here’s an honest side-by-side so you can choose with your eyes open.

Recommended

LED C9

  • The lookCrisp, bright warm-white glow
  • Cost per season~$3–$19
  • Bulb lifespanRated for multiple seasons
  • HeatCool to the touch

Efficient and durable — it costs pennies to run all season and is far less likely to need a mid-season fix. Our default for every package.

Classic Option

Incandescent C9

  • The lookDeeper, warmer amber glow
  • Cost per seasonRoughly 8–10× LED
  • Bulb lifespanShorter — burns out sooner
  • HeatRuns noticeably hot

The nostalgic, old-fashioned glow some homeowners grew up with — it just draws far more power and needs more mid-season bulb swaps.

LED is a few dollars for the whole season — not a few hundred. LED estimates based on ~$0.14/kWh all-in Cincinnati rates and typical LED C9 draw; incandescent figures scale that by the standard 8–10× wattage difference between incandescent and LED C9 bulbs. Your actual cost depends on your supply rate and hours run.
