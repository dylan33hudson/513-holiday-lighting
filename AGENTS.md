All repository guidance lives in **`CLAUDE.md`** at the repo root. Read it in full before editing.

The short version: `tokens.css` is the only source of truth for design values, no hex or raw color
may appear anywhere else, and the three usage rules (muted text only on the page ground, one solid
gold CTA per screen, hairlines use `--border-*`) are non-negotiable. `PRODUCT.md` carries the
content constraints.

Do not duplicate `CLAUDE.md` here. If guidance changes, change it there.
